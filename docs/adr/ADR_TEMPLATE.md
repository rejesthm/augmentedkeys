# ADR-NNN: Decision title

## Status

Proposed, in review, accepted, superseded, or rejected. State does not change without the required review record.

## Date

Use `YYYY-MM-DD` and add the decision timestamp when needed.

## Decision owners

Name the technical owner, product owner, required reviewers, and approval record.

## Context

Define the decision boundary, requirements, constraints, open questions, and why a decision is needed now. Separate confirmed facts from assumptions.

## Evidence and sources

Link authoritative sources, access dates, accepted research, spike plans and results, raw artifacts, tested commits, environments, and limitations. Label facts, measurements, inferences, and recommendations distinctly.

## Considered options

For each viable option, record how it was evaluated against the same criteria. Include the current approach and the option to defer where relevant; do not create a false comparison from untested claims.

## Decision

State the selected option and exact scope, including what remains undecided. Identify the owner approval that authorizes adoption or implementation.

## Consequences

Record benefits, costs, operational effects, portability, maintainability, failure modes, migration constraints, and work created by the decision.

## Performance and real-time implications

Record approved budgets, callback-boundary effects, allocation and synchronization behavior, lifecycle risks, measurement methods, physical-device coverage, and remaining uncertainty. State `Not applicable` only with a reason.

## Licensing implications

Link the exact `docs/research/LICENSE_LEDGER.md` entries and decisions for all adopted code or assets. Record commercial-use, redistribution, attribution, source, patent, trademark, and content-rights questions without offering unsupported conclusions.

## Verification plan

List acceptance criteria, exact commands and procedures, environments, device categories, configuration, artifact locations, failure testing, and required reviewers. A lower evidence level cannot replace a required higher one.

## Revisit triggers

Define measurable conditions that require re-evaluation, such as missed budgets, platform/API changes, unsupported devices, dependency or license changes, new failure evidence, or a change in product requirements.
